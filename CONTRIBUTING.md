# Contributing to GuineaDog and its utilities

Contributions are welcome! Before creating a new issue, please check the [existing issues](https://github.com/GuineaDog/GuineaDog/issues?q=is%3Aissue) (both open and closed) to see if your problem or suggestion has already been discussed.

When opening a new issue, please provide as much information as possible to help us understand the problem. Including **screenshots** or **terminal output copies** is highly encouraged as it greatly helps with debugging.

If your issue is new, feel free to open a [new issue](https://github.com/GuineaDog/GuineaDog/issues/new) or submit a [pull request](https://github.com/GuineaDog/GuineaDog/pulls).
